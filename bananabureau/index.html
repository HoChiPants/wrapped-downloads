◒ BANANA BUREAU

One object. Three ways to scan.

Banana Bureau can scan potatoes and other small objects; it does not require yellow peel or a banana name. Choose a pipeline, Select object, frame or draw a tight on-camera box and Use selection. Inspect Object only for unwanted hands or background. Generic segmentation, tracking, hand exclusion and depth checks are heuristic and may still fail. Select again corrects the same object; Restart begins a different capture.

Hybrid: turn the object gradually with overlapping views. Images align the views, measured depth refines camera placement and supplies surface points. Live LiDAR: keep the object still and move the phone around it. Selected depth points accumulate using AR camera tracking; no image file is saved and no photo reconstruction runs. Photo model: overlapping images create a mesh, with LiDAR helping establish scale. Mesh surfaces can be inferred rather than directly measured. This photo pathway failed a known-size synthetic geometry check in this build and is included for experimental comparison; substantial scale drift is possible.

Hybrid and Photo model allow up to 120 images and require twenty total inputs plus eight or three depth-bearing views respectively. Live LiDAR allows up to 300 depth frames and can review once usable points are mapped. Interrupted room tracking requires a restart for Live LiDAR. These are input limits, not coverage or accuracy certification.

Review shows the pipeline and geometry. Edit captured points is available for partial or invalid-shape geometry so you can cut out background before measurement checks. Canceling returns to review. Scan more preserves the current capture. An empty map provides an explanation instead of silently ignoring the button. Save a private draft before switching pipelines if you want to keep a result.

Results compares the last thirty local runs by pipeline/build, view counts, output points, processing time, outcome and failure reason. It contains no images, camera poses or point-cloud copy, is never uploaded and can be cleared. Private drafts show their pipeline. Comparing point counts or speed alone does not establish quality.

Hybrid and Photo model temporarily save background-blackened images, masks, depth and calibration locally while adding views, including after incomplete alignment. Accepting, closing or restarting deletes capture inputs; abandoned sessions are cleaned on app launch. Live LiDAR uses camera frames transiently for selection but saves no images. No image is uploaded or saved to Photos. Only uncolored geometry reaches drafts or the server.

Current geometry checks support small three-dimensional objects about 5–60 cm long and 0.8–20 cm wide. Length/width are estimates; girth and volume use rounded-section/taper models that may poorly describe a potato or irregular object. Full reconstruction, calibrated accuracy and solid volume remain unqualified. Inspect every result before publishing.

Names are automatic. Country/region and optional public postal codes work worldwide. Valid geometry and explicit consent produce immediate records/certificates; reports may hide misleading records. No human photo review. Delete records/account in the app. This static site has no cookies or trackers. Contact bananabureau@studiominden.org.
