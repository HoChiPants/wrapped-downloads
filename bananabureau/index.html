Field support.

Use the fixed crosshair and bottom + button to capture targets. Trace stem to tip, mark the width at the widest band, then capture at least eight separated points around that band for circumference. Use Map more of the peel to add surface patches along the banana.

Hold near the stem and brace your arm. Keep the fruit stationary while moving the phone. If your hand hides the band, save a partial scan or change grip and restart. The app does not track a freely moving banana or reconstruct hidden surfaces. Check length, width and circumference against a flexible tape.

A matte, contrasting surface or fixed stand is a stable fallback. Use bright diffuse light. Trace from stem to tip along the curve, holding still for each point. Check results against a flexible ruler. LiDAR requires a supported iPhone Pro or iPad Pro; use Photo + ruler otherwise.

Certificate links become available after review. You can remove your entries and account in Archive → Settings. Lost installation sessions cannot be recovered.
