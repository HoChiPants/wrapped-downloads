◒ BANANA BUREAU

Select. Scan. Crop. Share.

Banana Bureau is for adults 18 and older. On supported LiDAR iPhones, press the object to start one continuous scan. Finish scan, then Edit & continue. Drag in Rotate mode; choose Crop points for box cuts. Accept deletes temporary camera inputs and opens the draft.

At the bottom of the draft, use Save & Close or Publish after explicit consent. Draft deletion is only in Private drafts. Set a default angle by rotating the preview. Public records and contributor profiles show interactive LiDAR points at the top; share an image and the record link, without a caption.

Find users searches public generated aliases. Only the filing account can save a record view or request deletion. Deletion requests remove the record immediately and revoke live links. Recipients may retain already shared images.

Capture continues past 35; at least 20 retained 80+ images, eight with depth, enable Finish. A bounded 120-input set retains higher scores while protecting minimum depth. Confidence is outline quality, not accuracy or complete coverage. Scan again replaces finished captures with a fresh idle selection. Pause/options/review stop scheduling camera/depth/detection; model processing can still use compute while the camera is off.

Inputs stay protected/on-device and are removed on Accept, Close, Restart or abandonment cleanup. Only geometry, display angles, estimates and chosen public location are shared. Girth/volume are model estimates; missing surfaces and quarter-inch accuracy are not certified. Report/block controls and account deletion remain available.

Contact bananabureau@studiominden.org. No cookies, analytics or trackers. Browser 18+ confirmation uses session storage; no birth date or identity document is collected.
