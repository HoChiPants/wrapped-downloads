Field support.

Use the fixed crosshair and bottom + button to capture targets. Select Length (yellow), Width (mint) or Girth (pink) in any order. Each keeps its points when you switch. Trace stem to tip with two or more points, mark two opposite width edges, and capture at least eight separated points around one band for circumference. Redo clears only the selected tool; Undo and Restart are also available. Use Peel Map to add surface patches along the banana.

Hold near the stem and brace your arm. Keep the fruit stationary while moving the phone. If your hand hides the band, save a partial scan or change grip and restart. The app does not track a freely moving banana or reconstruct hidden surfaces. Check length, width and circumference against a flexible tape.

A matte, contrasting surface or fixed stand is a stable fallback. Use bright diffuse light. Trace from stem to tip along the curve, holding still for each point. Check results against a flexible ruler. LiDAR requires a supported iPhone Pro; use Photo + ruler otherwise.

Volume, length and width are the three ranking metrics. Volume is an estimate: cross-section area × curve length × ⅔ for a tapered shape. A complete girth band supplies its sampled area; width alone assumes a circular section. This is not a measured solid volume.
