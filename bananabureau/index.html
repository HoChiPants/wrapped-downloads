◒ BANANA BUREAU

Build the object as you circle.

A LiDAR-capable iPhone Pro is required. Aim at clear peel near the middle of a steady banana and press Start once. The live display stays white points on black as you circle with the phone. New views add to the same object in one fixed physical meter scale; they do not replace or resize the earlier points. A contrasting background and brighter light help isolation.

Pause and Resume keep the same object and scale. Finish freezes a valid object for publishing. Keep the banana stationary; views that fail to align are rejected. If tracking is interrupted, preserved points can be finished, but Restart before adding views from a new coordinate frame. View-angle counts indicate observed directions, not proof of a complete or sealed surface.

No underside photograph or circumference pass is required. Hidden surfaces remain missing. Girth and volume are labeled model estimates from visible width, not measured hidden geometry. Isolation is heuristic and is not guaranteed banana recognition.

Names are automatic. Choose a country/region and an optional ZIP/postal code worldwide; leave it blank where none is used. Valid geometry publishes immediately and creates a certificate. Reports may hide a record. No photo is uploaded or stored. Delete records or your installation account in Archive → Settings.
