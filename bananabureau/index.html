◒ BANANA BUREAU

Turn the fruit. Keep the peel.

On a supported LiDAR iPhone Pro, frame the whole yellow banana and check the Included Image preview. It should show peel on black, with the hand and floor excluded. Tap Scan Banana and turn the banana slowly, holding each new side briefly. Change your grip to expose hidden peel. Distinct overlapping views save automatically; Capture View requests a shot. Pause preserves views and enables Undo. The handheld flow has no floor box, room lock or orbit dial.

Peel isolation uses color and shape, not guaranteed banana recognition. Green, very brown or occluded fruit, glare and low light can cause failure. Keep the whole fruit in view; try 20–120 cm for LiDAR depth. Repeated views do not count as progress. Twenty accepted views are required to try reconstruction, with at most 80 temporary views. Neither count proves full coverage.

Reconstruct matches the isolated images on-device and uses LiDAR depth for scale; it does not reuse AR room poses or gravity as constraints on a turning banana. Background pixels are blackened before saving temporary images. Images, masks, depth files and models are removed after processing/cancellation/failure; abandoned sessions are cleaned on app launch. No image is uploaded or saved to Photos.

Inspect the reconstructed white geometry on black. Rotate, Box Select and Cut Selected remove unwanted points through depth. Undo restores them. Use Points applies an edit to a protected draft; Cancel discards changes. Invalid geometry can stay private but cannot publish. Length and width are geometry estimates; girth and volume use a rounded-section/taper model.

Handheld reconstruction and physical measurement remain experimental. A local test on screen-recording-derived images produced a distorted partial mesh; it does not qualify a full real-banana scan or calibrated scale. Native photo capture, full surface coverage and size accuracy require device testing. Do not publish distorted or misleading geometry.

Names are automatic. Worldwide country/region and optional public postal codes remain. Plausible geometry and explicit consent produce immediate records and certificates. Reports can hide misleading records. Delete records/account in Archive → Settings. This site uses no cookies or trackers. Contact bananabureau@studiominden.org.
