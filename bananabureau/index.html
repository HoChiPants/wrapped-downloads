◒ BANANA BUREAU

One continuous 3D scan.

On a supported LiDAR iPhone Pro, frame the whole yellow banana and check Banana only. Keep fingers and surroundings excluded. Start scan, then turn gradually with overlapping peel visible. Change grip to expose the bottom and tilt toward both tips. There are no separate side/top sections. Pause and Continue keep the same scan; Capture now adds a view, Undo removes the last paused view and Restart deletes the capture.

Review 3D scan aligns the camera images on the iPhone, then merges actual LiDAR surface samples from matched views using camera calibration. It does not export only the vertices of a photo mesh. At least twenty images and eight depth-bearing views are required to try alignment, up to 120 temporary views. Image-only views can help alignment but do not supply missing depth. No hidden surface or arbitrary scale is invented.

The rotatable map shows how many views aligned and contributed depth. If they do not surround the body or tilt toward both ends, Use scan and edit stays unavailable: Scan more continues the same capture. These are heuristic direction/shape checks, not proof of complete geometry or accuracy. Inspect every surface. Green/brown peel, glare, shadows, thin tips, smooth texture and occluding fingers can still prevent isolation or alignment.

Camera inputs are background-blackened before temporary saving. Images, masks, depth and calibration stay on-device while the current scan is active, including when continuing after an incomplete result. Using, closing or restarting deletes those temporary inputs; abandoned sessions are cleaned on app launch. No image is uploaded or saved to Photos. Only uncolored geometry is retained or uploaded.

Rotate, Box select, Cut selected and Undo cut let you clean the geometry before applying it to a protected draft. Selections cut through depth. Length and width are geometry estimates; volume and girth remain rounded-section/taper models. Full physical handheld reconstruction and measurement accuracy remain unqualified; software tests and direction counts cannot certify a complete scan.

Names are automatic. Worldwide country/region and optional public postal codes remain. Plausible geometry and explicit consent produce immediate records and certificates; reports may hide misleading records. Delete records/account in Archive and Settings.
