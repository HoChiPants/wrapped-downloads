Select. Circle. Reconstruct.

Use a supported LiDAR iPhone Pro. Support the banana so it stays still while you move the phone. Tap Select Banana, adjust the 3D box around just the fruit, then Start Orbit. Keep your hand, knee and background outside the box where possible. Move slowly and follow the capture dial and lighting, distance and speed feedback. Show Camera helps you aim; Show Points returns to a monochrome capture display. Inspect pauses capture.

Complete an orbit, then add another pass from a higher or lower angle. To capture the underside, choose Flip for Underside, reposition the banana and select its box again. Low-texture or shiny peel and moving supports can prevent reliable reconstruction or stitching. Partial capture may leave missing surfaces. At least 20 captures are required to try reconstruction; that count does not certify completeness.

Reconstruct builds a mesh entirely on the iPhone using LiDAR and temporary images. Images, textures and checkpoints are removed after processing, cancellation or failure, and abandoned captures are cleaned on the next app launch. They are never uploaded or saved to Photos. Only uncolored geometry is retained. Reconstruction can take a few minutes.

In Edit / Rotate Object Points, rotate unwanted geometry clear of the banana, choose Box Select and draw around it. Cut Selected removes the highlighted points through the object depth; Undo Cut restores them. Use Points applies the edit; Cancel discards it. You can reopen protected drafts and edit again before publishing.

Length and width come from reconstructed geometry. Girth and volume remain rounded-section/tapered-shape estimates, not certified solid volume. Device capture, isolation, registration and size accuracy need physical testing. No reference card is required.

Names are automatic. Choose a country/region and an optional ZIP/postal code worldwide. Geometry checks and explicit consent produce an immediate public record and certificate. Reports may hide misleading records. The server accepts geometry only. Delete records or your installation account in Archive → Settings.
