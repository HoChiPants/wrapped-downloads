Your capture images stay on your iPhone.

On-device foreground segmentation, tracking, hand exclusion and LiDAR depth help isolate the object you select. Background-blackened images, masks, depth, calibration and estimated camera poses are temporary inputs for local reconstruction and editing. They are not uploaded.

Inputs remain protected and excluded from backups while the scan is active, including Captured views editing. Crop edits link measured image regions and object points inside that active editor; image-only pixels have no corresponding geometry. Cancel discards editor changes. Accepting, closing or restarting deletes temporary capture inputs; abandoned captures are cleaned on app launch. Private drafts retain geometry and local scan provenance, not input images. Captured input images are not saved to Photos. You may choose to save the generated stats card from the system share sheet; the app does not read your photo library.

Optional Highlight shows the detector's current frame. Aligned coverage details are available during review. The app does not upload images, video, RGB payloads, raw room meshes, camera poses or GPS coordinates.

A protected, backup-excluded local scan history keeps up to thirty summaries: build/date, capture counts, output-point count, processing time, outcome/failure and available estimates. It contains no image, camera pose or cloud copy, is never uploaded or automatically attached to support mail, and can be cleared in Scan history.

The server stores uncolored XYZ points, estimates, a generated record name and contributor alias, selected country/region, optional postal code, reports and a hashed installation token. Publishing makes geometry, estimates and chosen location public after explicit consent. Drafts stay on the iPhone. Sharing is initiated by you through the system share sheet: the selected destination receives a generated geometry image and public record link, with no extra caption. No captured photo, private draft, input image, camera pose or account token is included.

Owner deletion requests are completed immediately in the app and revoke live record, point-cloud, preview and certificate links. Previously shared images may remain with recipients. Minimal deletion-request IDs and timestamps remain until account deletion; geometry is removed. Public contributor search shows aliases and published records only. Default 3D viewpoints are stored with geometry. An 18+ confirmation is kept locally on the device/browser; no birth date or identity document is collected. Delete records/account in the app; public certificate links are revoked with the record. There is no email recovery. Older beta photos were removed from active storage; provider backups may retain earlier data until retention expires. Hosting diagnostics may include IP addresses. Software checks do not establish object identity, calibrated accuracy, complete coverage or physical ownership. This static site has no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
