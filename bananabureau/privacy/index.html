Object images stay on your iPhone.

Continuous scanning isolates yellow peel in camera images and captures LiDAR depth. Background pixels are blackened before temporary image files are saved. Images are matched on-device to estimate object-relative view positions. Actual depth samples from matched views are then merged using camera calibration. AR room poses, gravity, GPS and serial identifiers are not used as object-motion matching inputs. Isolation, alignment and coverage checks are heuristic; inspect Banana only and the final rotatable map.

Temporary images, masks, depth and calibration remain in protected, backup-excluded app storage while the current scan is active. Adding more views after failed alignment or an incomplete review keeps those inputs in the same session. Using, closing or restarting the scan deletes its temporary inputs; abandoned sessions are cleaned on app launch. Nothing is saved to Photos. No image, video, RGB payload, raw room mesh, camera pose or GPS coordinate is uploaded. Only uncolored object-relative geometry is retained in drafts or submitted.

The server stores XYZ object points, estimated dimensions, a generated name/contributor label, selected country/region, optional postal code, reports and a hashed installation token. Publishing makes that geometry, estimates and chosen location public. Private drafts stay on the iPhone in protected, backup-excluded storage. Older beta photos were removed from active storage; hosting backups may retain older data until retention expires.

Software checks do not establish banana identity, complete surface coverage, calibrated accuracy or a sealed volume. Delete records or your installation account in the app. No email recovery. Hosting diagnostics may include IP addresses. This static site has no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Contact bananabureau@studiominden.org.
