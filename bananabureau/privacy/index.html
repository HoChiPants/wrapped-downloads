Selected-object images stay on your iPhone.

On-device foreground segmentation, tracking, hand exclusion and depth checks help isolate the object you select without requiring a particular color or object name. Live LiDAR uses camera frames transiently and retains selected geometry without saving image files. Hybrid and Photo model save background-blackened images, masks, depth and calibration temporarily for local alignment/reconstruction. Estimated camera poses are used locally and are not uploaded.

Temporary capture inputs remain in protected, backup-excluded storage while adding views after a failed or incomplete review. Accepting, closing or restarting deletes those inputs; abandoned sessions are cleaned on app launch. Nothing is saved to Photos. No image, video, RGB payload, raw room mesh, camera pose or GPS coordinate is uploaded. Only uncolored object-relative geometry is retained in private drafts or submitted.

A protected, backup-excluded local comparison history keeps up to thirty pipeline runs: pipeline/build, date, view counts, output-point count, processing time, outcome/failure reason and available dimension estimates. It stores no image, camera pose or point-cloud copy and is never uploaded or automatically attached to support mail. Clear it in Results. Private drafts may retain the pipeline and local trial identifier.

The server stores XYZ object points, estimated dimensions, a generated name/contributor label, selected country/region, optional postal code, reports and a hashed installation token. Publishing makes geometry, estimates and chosen location public. Private drafts stay protected on the iPhone. Older beta photos were removed from active storage; provider backups may retain earlier data until their retention expires.

Software checks do not establish object identity, complete surface coverage, calibrated accuracy or a sealed volume. Delete records/installation account in the app; no email recovery. Hosting diagnostics may include IP addresses. This static site has no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Contact bananabureau@studiominden.org.
