Object points. No photos.

Camera frames are processed transiently on your iPhone to isolate a depth-connected, color-similar object. No camera image, photo, video, RGB color, room mesh or GPS coordinate is uploaded. The server stores localized XYZ object points, estimated dimensions, a generated record name and contributor label, your selected country or region, an optional postal code, reports and a hashed installation token.

Publishing makes your object points, estimates and selected region/postal code public. Unsubmitted drafts stay on your iPhone in protected, backup-excluded storage. This beta migration removes previously stored photos from active server storage and removes photos from private drafts when loaded. Hosting backups may retain older data until provider retention expires.

Automatic geometry checks do not verify fruit identity, physical accuracy or a sealed volume. Delete records or your installation account in the app. No email recovery is available. Hosting diagnostics may include IP addresses. This static site uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
