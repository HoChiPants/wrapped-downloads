Geometry stays. Images do not leave.

Optional manual area capture can temporarily reconstruct nearby surfaces on-device when object detection is unavailable. The larger preview exists only in the private editor, not in drafts or API submissions. Trim it to the object before Use Points; export localizes the geometry and enforces object-scale and point-count bounds. You must inspect the result because software does not establish banana identity.

On supported LiDAR iPhones, Apple Object Capture combines LiDAR and temporary camera images to reconstruct a 3D surface entirely on the device. Images, textures, reconstruction files and checkpoints stay in a protected, backup-excluded temporary directory. The app removes them after reconstruction, cancellation or failure; captures abandoned by an app termination are cleaned on the next launch. Nothing is saved to Photos. Only uncolored XYZ object geometry is retained for editing and drafts.

No camera image, photo, video, RGB payload, room mesh or GPS coordinate is uploaded. The server stores localized XYZ object points, estimated dimensions, a generated record name and contributor label, your selected country or region, an optional postal code, reports and a hashed installation token.

Publishing makes your object geometry, estimates and selected region/postal code public. Unsubmitted drafts stay on your iPhone in protected, backup-excluded storage. Earlier beta photos were removed from active server storage and are removed from old private drafts when loaded. Hosting backups may retain older data until provider retention expires.

Automatic geometry checks do not verify fruit identity, physical accuracy, complete coverage or a sealed volume. Reconstruction may miss or infer surfaces. Delete records or your installation account in the app. No email recovery is available. Hosting diagnostics may include IP addresses.
