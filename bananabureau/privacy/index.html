Object images stay on your iPhone.

The handheld capture path isolates yellow peel in camera images and uses LiDAR depth for scale. Background pixels are blackened before temporary image files are saved. Image masks, depth maps and focal metadata are used for on-device reconstruction; camera world poses, gravity, GPS and serial identifiers are not supplied as handheld matching inputs. A color/shape heuristic can miss fruit or include unwanted pixels, so inspect the included-image preview and final geometry.

Temporary images, masks, depth files and generated model files stay in protected, backup-excluded app storage. They are removed after processing, cancellation or failure, with abandoned sessions cleaned on the next app launch. Nothing is saved to Photos. No image, video, RGB payload, raw room mesh, camera pose or GPS coordinate is uploaded. Only uncolored object-relative geometry is retained in drafts or submitted.

The server stores XYZ object points, estimated dimensions, a generated name/contributor label, selected country/region, optional postal code, reports and a hashed installation token. Publishing makes that geometry, estimates and chosen location public. Private drafts stay on the iPhone in protected, backup-excluded storage. Older beta photos were removed from active storage; hosting backups may retain older data until retention expires.

Software checks do not establish banana identity, complete coverage, calibrated accuracy or a sealed volume. Delete records or your installation account in the app. No email recovery. Hosting diagnostics may include IP addresses. This static site has no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Contact bananabureau@studiominden.org.
