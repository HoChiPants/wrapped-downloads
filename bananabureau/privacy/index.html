Privacy, plainly.

We store your chosen alias, fruit photo, selected country or state, dimensions, trace points, captured object-relative surface patches, reports, and a hashed installation token. We do not request GPS coordinates. Photo metadata is stripped before storage. Approved entries, surface maps and certificates are public. Surface patches are limited to the areas you capture; the app does not upload an AR room mesh. Pending entries are visible to you and moderators.

Unsubmitted drafts, including photos and trace points, stay in protected storage on your iPhone and are excluded from device backups. They are not uploaded automatically. Resume or discard them in Archive; successful submission removes the local draft. Deleting your installation account also removes its local drafts.

Delete an entry or your installation account in the app to remove its database records and photos and revoke its certificate. Infrastructure backups may persist until provider retention expires. Reinstalling can lose access; there is no email-based recovery in this version.

API traffic and hosting diagnostics may include IP addresses. This static site uses no cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. A supporting hand may appear in a banana photo. Do not upload faces or personal information.
