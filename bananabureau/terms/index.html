Fruit rules.

Submit only banana photos you own and measurements you believe are honest. No faces, nudity, abusive content, personal information, or stolen images. A supporting hand is okay. You grant us permission to host and display your submission until you delete it. Entries require moderation and may be removed. Report inappropriate or misleading entries in the app.

Measurements are estimates. LiDAR labels describe submitted sensor data, not anti-cheat verification. Certificates are playful community records, not professional metrology, government documents, or guarantees. Do not use the app for medical, safety, or commercial measurements.

Use is intended for ages 13 and up. No in-app purchases. Contact bananabureau@studiominden.org for assistance or content reports.
