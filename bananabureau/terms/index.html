Honest object points.

Capture only the object and confirm that the point preview excludes your hand, knee and surrounding room. Publish only object geometry you intend to share. The server accepts object geometry only; no photographs. Country and optional postal code are self-selected and are public when supplied.

Names and contributor labels are generated automatically. Bounded, geometrically plausible point-cloud records publish automatically with a community certificate. This does not validate fruit identity, prevent fabricated data or establish calibrated accuracy. Girth and volume use a rounded-section/tapered-shape model. Reports can hide misleading records pending investigation; contributor blocking and account/record deletion are available.

Intended for ages 13 and up. Do not use estimates for medical, safety or commercial decisions. No in-app purchases. Contact bananabureau@studiominden.org.
