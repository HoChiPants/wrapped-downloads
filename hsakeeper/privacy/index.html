HSA Keeper privacy policy.

Effective September 27, 2026

Records and account information

HSA Keeper processes authentication account details, expenses, reimbursement status, notes, receipt metadata, uploaded documents, billing status, and technical information needed to operate and secure the service. Records you enter may contain sensitive financial and health-related information.

Purpose and providers

This information supports account access, recordkeeping, document storage, reimbursement planning, exports, billing, support, and security. Authentication, infrastructure, storage, payment, app-store, and email providers process information to provide these services. We do not sell personal expense records or uploaded documents to advertisers or data brokers.

Permissions

Camera access is used when you choose to photograph a receipt. Photo and file selection gives the app access to the items you choose to attach. Uploaded receipts and records are processed by the HSA Keeper service; they are not stored only on your device.

Support

If you contact support, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. The in-app draft adds only app name, marketing version, build number, OS version, and device model. It does not silently attach receipts, logs, account data, or identifiers.

Retention and deletion

Records remain available until you delete them or your account. The Account screen offers export and permanent account deletion. Subscription cancellation and account deletion are separate. Exported files, emails, and copies held by services you choose must be managed separately.

Web analytics

The separate hsakeeper.org website may use Google Analytics when enabled, collecting website usage and device information. Saved expenses and receipt contents are not sent to Google Analytics. This GitHub Pages support site has no cookies or analytics. GitHub Pages may process technical requests for hosting and security.

Your choices

You can review, export, edit, and delete your records in the service and manage subscriptions through the relevant store or billing controls. Apple processes App Store and subscription information under its own privacy terms.

Contact

Email hsakeeper@studiominden.org for privacy questions or account-deletion help. We will update this page when the policy changes.
