HSA Keeper terms of use.

Effective September 27, 2026

The service

HSA Keeper helps organize HSA expense records, receipts, reimbursement status, and exports. You remain responsible for record accuracy, tax eligibility, reimbursement decisions, and keeping additional records required by law or your HSA custodian.

Subscriptions

Paid access enables adding, editing, uploading, and email-delivery features after introductory access ends. The purchase screen shows the current price, billing period, and any applicable introductory offer. Apple subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled through Apple subscription settings. Manage web subscriptions through the web billing portal.

After access ends

Existing records remain readable and exportable after a trial or subscription ends. Adding, editing, uploading, and email-delivery features pause until access is restored.

Your account

Keep your sign-in credentials secure. You can export data and permanently delete your account in HSA Keeper. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the service, attempt unauthorized access, upload unlawful content, interfere with others, or violate another person’s rights.

No professional advice

HSA Keeper is a recordkeeping tool and does not provide medical, tax, legal, financial, or investment advice. Consult a qualified professional for advice about your circumstances.

Availability and changes

Uninterrupted service is not guaranteed. Features and these terms may change as the service develops; material changes will be communicated through the service when appropriate. Keep independent copies of records you need.
