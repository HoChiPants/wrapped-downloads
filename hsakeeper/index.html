HSA Keeper support.

Effective September 27, 2026

Keep your HSA records organized

HSA Keeper organizes expenses, receipts, reimbursement status, and exports. It does not determine whether an expense qualifies for reimbursement and does not provide medical, tax, legal, financial, or investment advice.

Get help

Email hsakeeper@studiominden.org for help with sign-in, expenses, receipt attachments, exports, or subscriptions. Describe what happened and the app version. Do not send passwords, medical details, or receipt copies unless requested and you choose to share them.

Receipts and exports

Use Expenses to add a record and attach a camera photo, selected photo, or file. In Account, export a tax year or all account data. Keep an independent backup of records you need.

Apple subscriptions

Account includes Restore Apple Purchases and Manage Apple Subscription. Apple displays the current localized price before purchase. After access ends, existing records remain readable and exportable; adding and editing require active access.

Delete your account

Account → Delete Account permanently removes app data and the sign-in account. Export first if you need a copy. Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage it separately in your Apple subscription settings.
