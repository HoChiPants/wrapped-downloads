Privacy policy.

Effective September 20, 2026

The short version

NotifySpend is designed to keep your expense information on your device. We do not operate a NotifySpend account system, bank connection, analytics service, advertising service, or developer-operated transaction backend. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Information you choose to store

NotifySpend can store transaction details that you enter or import, such as merchant, amount, currency, date, category, account hint, note, transaction type, recurring schedules, and a notification or message’s source text when you choose to retain it. Receipt images are processed on-device to recognize text. The app stores this information locally on your device by default.

Shortcuts and notifications

NotifySpend cannot read your notifications or messages directly. A personal Apple Shortcuts automation can explicitly pass text or structured transaction details into NotifySpend. You choose whether to create an automation and what it passes. Apple Shortcuts and the source app may have their own privacy practices.

Private iCloud sync

If you turn on private iCloud sync, transactions, retained source text, review items, categories, merchant rules, and schedules sync through your private iCloud account to your own devices. Apple provides this service under its own terms and privacy policy. Turning sync off stops future syncing; it does not itself erase data already stored in iCloud. You can delete your app data in NotifySpend.

Optional cloud parsing

NotifySpend can use Apple’s on-device model on supported devices. It also offers an optional cloud fallback only if you enter your own compatible provider endpoint, model, and API key and turn the feature on. When enabled, unfamiliar notification text—which can include transaction and account details—is sent directly to the provider you selected. That provider’s privacy policy, retention practices, and pricing apply. Your API key is stored in your device Keychain. Studio Minden does not provide or operate that endpoint through NotifySpend.

Camera and photo library

If you choose to scan or select a receipt, NotifySpend accesses the camera or selected photo only to recognize text on-device and prepare a transaction for your review. NotifySpend does not upload receipt images to Studio Minden.

Sharing, tracking, cookies, and retention

NotifySpend does not include advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics. It does not automatically share transactions. CSV export occurs only when you choose it. This static support site does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking technologies. Your data remains on your device or, if enabled, in your private iCloud account until you delete it, remove the app, or manage it through your device and iCloud settings. Device backups may retain app data according to your backup settings.

Children

NotifySpend is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post a revised effective date on this page. For privacy questions, email notifyspend@studiominden.org. Do not send account numbers, bank alerts, API keys, passwords, one-time codes, or CSV exports with financial data.
