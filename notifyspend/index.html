NotifySpend.

Private, local-first expense tracking for iPhone and iPad.

Track spending on your terms

NotifySpend keeps transactions on your device by default. You can enter them yourself, scan a receipt, or use a personal Shortcuts automation that explicitly passes a bank notification or message into the app. No NotifySpend account or bank login is required.

Make NotifySpend yours

Core expense tracking stays free. The optional one-time Supporter Pack adds alternate icons, custom themes, spending and plan widgets, category budgets with rollover and history, and savings goals with dated contributions. Every support amount unlocks the same features. Budgets and goals stay on your device; they do not move money.
