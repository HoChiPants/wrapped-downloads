NotifySpend.

Private, local-first expense tracking for iPhone and iPad.

Track spending on your terms

NotifySpend keeps transactions on your device by default. You can enter them yourself, scan a receipt, or use a personal Shortcuts automation that explicitly passes a bank notification or message into the app. No NotifySpend account or bank login is required.
