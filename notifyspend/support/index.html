Before contacting us

  • Update NotifySpend and your device to the latest available version.
  • Close and reopen NotifySpend, then try the action again.
  • For a Shortcuts import, verify that the automation runs immediately and passes its incoming Notification or Message as Shortcut Input.
  • Open More → Settings → Shortcuts Diagnostics. No entry means the automation did not reach NotifySpend; an input-missing or rejected entry helps identify the next step.

Shortcuts and bank alerts

NotifySpend cannot read notifications or messages by itself. You choose what a personal Apple Shortcuts automation sends to the app. Personal automations are managed by Apple Shortcuts, can vary by iOS version and region, and must be created on your device.

To test your setup without using a real bank alert, open More → Settings → Test a Notification, schedule the built-in test alert, and connect a NotifySpend notification automation in Shortcuts. You can also paste a synthetic alert into the in-app test screen.

Data and privacy

Transactions stay on your device by default. If you enable private iCloud sync, your data syncs through your own Apple account. You can delete all app data in More → Settings. Read the full NotifySpend privacy policy and terms of use.

Never email API keys, full card or account numbers, one-time codes, passwords, unredacted bank alerts, or CSV exports containing financial data.

Supporter Pack

The optional one-time Supporter Pack adds icons, custom themes, spending and plan widgets, monthly category budgets, and savings goals. Every support amount unlocks the same features. Core transaction tracking and existing appearance choices remain free. To restore a purchase, open More → Settings → Support NotifySpend → Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Budgets and widgets

Budgets use the current category name. A category with a budget cannot be deleted until you remove its budget; this prevents unexpected changes to budget spending and history. Budgets and goals stay on this device and are not part of transaction CSV exports or private iCloud sync.

To configure widgets, open More → Settings → Spending Widgets, enable amounts, and select the plans you want to share. Touch and hold each widget, choose Edit Widget, and choose its plan. Names are shared only when you enable the separate name option. Budget month changes use the last saved expenses and limits for up to a year; new imports refresh the saved totals. Check the update date, and open the app if values look stale. iOS controls widget refresh timing.
