Privacy policy.

Effective October 14, 2026. Token Widget is operated by Austin Stephens.

What the app does

Token Widget connects to your eligible Codex account to display allowance, reset information and reported token statistics. Preview mode uses sample data and does not connect an account.

Account connection and usage

You authenticate on OpenAI’s website. We do not receive your OpenAI password. Our Railway-hosted service stores authorization credentials, account email and plan information, session identifiers, and cached usage summaries, including available allowance, reset information and reported token statistics. Service records are encrypted at rest. The device stores its service token in the iOS Keychain and shares usage snapshots with its widget.

Preview mode

Preview mode displays fictional sample data without connecting an account or consuming allowance. It does not populate installed widgets. Companion generation has been removed; the service rejects generation requests and removes legacy generated results during authenticated reads and cleanup sweeps.

What we do not collect

The app does not retrieve your conversation-history text, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or precise location. We do not include advertising or third-party behavioral analytics, sell personal data, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites. No content-generation prompts are accepted in this release.

Retention and deletion

Service sessions expire 30 days after creation and are removed by the service’s expiry cleanup. Disconnect account deletes that service connection, credentials, cached account usage. If cleanup cannot finish offline, the app retains the information needed to retry and displays that state. Deletion from Token Widget does not delete data separately retained by OpenAI or delete your OpenAI account. The app removes legacy companion data while preserving account credentials and usage. Local settings and usage snapshots remain on the device until removed with the app; device backups are managed by your device settings.

Service providers and support

Railway hosts the connection service. OpenAI provides authentication and account usage. GitHub hosts this website. Support email is routed through our domain mail provider to the Studio Minden Gmail inbox. If you contact support, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request. Network infrastructure receives ordinary connection metadata, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the service. Do not include passwords, device codes, authorization tokens or private conversation content in support requests.

Your choices

You can use preview mode, disconnect each device’s service connection, and manage your OpenAI account directly. For support or privacy questions, see the support page. Policy changes will be published here with a revised effective date.
