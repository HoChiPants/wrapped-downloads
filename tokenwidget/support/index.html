A little more headroom.

Token Widget shows your available Codex allowance, reset times, and customizable companions on iPhone and iPad. Available metrics depend on your account. Widget refresh timing is controlled by iOS.

Contact support

Email tokenwidget@studiominden.org. In the app, open Account → Contact Support to include the app version, build, OS version and device model in a draft you can review before sending. No logs or account data are attached automatically.

Connection help

Choose Connect Codex, open the OpenAI sign-in page and enter the displayed device code. Enable device-code login in your ChatGPT security settings if requested. Return to the app after approval. Never send passwords, device codes or authorization tokens to support.

Disconnect and deletion

Account → Disconnect account removes this service connection, stored authorization, cached usage and server generation results. If offline, retry cleanup when connected. Local companion data may remain. This does not delete your OpenAI account.

Privacy questions

Use the same support address for privacy or deletion requests. See our privacy policy and terms of use.
