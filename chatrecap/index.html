ChatRecap support

Effective October 5, 2026

Your conversations, recapped privately

ChatRecap, previously named MessagesWrapped and Wrapped, turns selected conversations into story-style recaps on your iPhone. The new name keeps the same app identity and saved results.

Get help

Choose Contact Support on the privacy or saved-results screen, or email the address below. The draft includes only app name, marketing version, build number, OS version, and device model. Review it before sending. Do not send private conversations, account credentials, or payment details.

Import a conversation

Connect to the Wrapped Mac companion on your local network, or import a WhatsApp TXT/ZIP, GroupMe export, or supported JSON/CSV file. For WhatsApp, export a chat without media and select ChatRecap in the share sheet. Confirm the participants before analysis.

Mac setup instructions · Download Wrapped Mac

Purchases and saved results

Use Restore Purchases on the Unlimited screen to restore an eligible purchase with the same Apple account. Recaps are stored locally; deleting the app can remove its local data. Review each card before sharing.
