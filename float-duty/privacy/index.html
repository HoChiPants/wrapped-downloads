Privacy policy

Float Duty · Last updated October 6, 2026

Local game data

Solo checkpoints, delivery medals, postcards, and best times are stored locally when saving is unlocked on iOS. Free-play progress is held in memory for the session. The app does not offer accounts or cloud progress sync. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your settings.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases. The app uses Apple-verified purchase information to determine whether saving is unlocked. Float Duty does not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Local multiplayer

Same-network co-op sends gameplay state and player input directly between devices. Network addresses are needed to connect to the host. This is not sent to a Studio Minden game server.

Support and diagnostics

If you email support, we receive the message and email address you choose to send. The support draft contains app name, version, build, operating-system version, and device model. No logs or game data are attached automatically. Apple may process beta feedback and diagnostics when you use TestFlight or enable platform diagnostic sharing.

Ads, analytics, and website cookies

The app does not include advertising or a Studio Minden analytics service. This static support website uses no cookies or tracking scripts.

Deletion and contact

Deleting the app removes its local progress from the device; system backups are managed separately in your device settings. Purchase history remains with Apple. For support-data questions, contact float-duty@studiominden.org.
