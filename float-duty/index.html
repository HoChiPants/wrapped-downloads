Float Duty

A precious balloon. A small crew. One more delivery.

Game support

Float Duty is a landscape physics delivery game for iPhone and iPad. Guide Pip past padded obstacles and red hazards. Touch arrows move your rigger; Jump / Float helps with gaps; Down and Up adjust the tether.

The mobile campaign includes Meadow Mail, Harbor Lights, and Summit Express, each with eight sections. Classic, Headwinds, and Fragile Cargo challenges add delivery medals and postcards.

Saving and purchases

All routes can be played free. On iOS, an optional one-time saving unlock retains solo checkpoints, medals, and best times between sessions. Checkpoints and retries during an active run remain free. The App Store shows the price before purchase.

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that bought the unlock. Restoring the purchase restores saving access, not progress from another device. Progress stays on this device; LAN sessions are not saved.

Need help?

Email float-duty@studiominden.org. Include your app version and what happened. Do not send passwords or payment details. The in-app support draft includes basic app, OS, and device-model diagnostics for you to review.

For co-op, use the same Wi-Fi network, allow Local Network access, and enter the host IP. Co-op uses the original Meadow Mail route. Internet matchmaking is not included.
