Optional saving unlock

On iOS, the saving unlock is a one-time non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. Apple presents the applicable price and processes the purchase. All game routes and active-session retries remain available without buying saving.

Unlocked saving keeps solo checkpoints and delivery records on the current device. It does not provide cloud sync, multiplayer-session restoration, or recovery of deleted local data. Restore Purchases restores the entitlement using the purchasing Apple Account. Refunds and purchase issues are handled through Apple’s applicable process.
