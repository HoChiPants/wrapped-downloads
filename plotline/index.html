Plotline support.

Effective September 21, 2026

Your stories, on your device

Plotline for iPhone and iPad helps you catch book ideas, develop characters, and arrange scenes across story threads. Write locally without an account or a running server.

Get help

Email plotline@studiominden.org, or choose Settings → Contact Support in the app. The draft includes only app name, version, build, OS version, and device model. Review it before sending; please do not send private manuscripts or account credentials.

Save a book to iCloud Drive

Open Notebook → Save book to Files and choose iCloud Drive. This saves a readable JSON snapshot of the current book, including ideas, scenes, characters, threads, chapters, and locations. Save again after changes: this is a manual backup, not automatic synchronization.

Open a backup

Use Notebook → Import book from Files. When the book already exists, Plotline keeps it and imports a separate copy. Imports are limited to 20 MB. Keep your latest backup before deleting the app or changing devices.

Sharing and Photos

Sharing is optional and uses Apple’s share sheet or Messages. Story cards show the project title, subtitle, thread names, and planning counts, not scene notes. Saving an image to Photos asks for add-only permission. Check the preview before sharing.

Privacy and terms

Read the privacy policy and terms of use. This static support site does not use cookies, tracking, or analytics.
