Plotline privacy policy.

Effective September 21, 2026

Your writing stays local

Plotline stores your book projects, ideas, characters, scenes, preferences, and notes on your device. The current local workspace does not require an account and does not automatically upload manuscripts to Studio Minden. It includes no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Files and iCloud Drive

You may export a book using Apple’s Files picker and choose iCloud Drive, on-device storage, or another provider. Exports contain your writing in readable JSON and are not password-protected by Plotline. The selected storage provider processes those files under its own terms. Studio Minden does not receive your backups. Importing reads only the file you select. Automatic cross-device sync is not provided.

Sharing and permissions

You decide when to share a story card through Messages or another service. Cards may include your book title, subtitle, thread names, and planning counts. Saving a card to Photos uses add-only permission; Plotline does not read your photo library. The app does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone, or health data.

Support email

If you contact support, Studio Minden receives your email address, message, and anything you choose to include. The in-app draft pre-fills only app name, marketing version, integer build number, OS version, and device model. It does not silently attach book content, logs, account data, or device identifiers. Support email is handled through Studio Minden’s email service and used to respond to your request. Correspondence is kept only as needed to handle support and legitimate recordkeeping; you can request deletion at plotline@studiominden.org.

Apple and other services

Apple may process TestFlight feedback, crash information, App Store records, and iCloud data under Apple’s privacy terms and your device settings. Services you choose for sharing or file storage apply their own policies.

Deletion and control

Delete individual ideas or scenes in the app. Deleting Plotline removes its local app data. Exported files, shared images, emails, and copies in iCloud or device backups must be managed separately with their respective services.

This website

This static support site does not use cookies, advertising, or analytics, so no cookie consent banner is needed. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical request information to deliver and secure it.

Contact and changes

Email plotline@studiominden.org for privacy questions. We will update this page and its effective date when the policy changes.
