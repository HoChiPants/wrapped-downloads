Email Tidekeeper support Report a public bug

Before contacting us

  • Update Tidekeeper and your device to the latest available version.
  • Close and reopen the app.
  • Restart your device if the issue continues.

Tidekeeper saves progress on your device. Deleting the app also deletes its locally stored progress.
