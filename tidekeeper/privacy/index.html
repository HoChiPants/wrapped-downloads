Tidekeeper privacy policy.

Effective August 28, 2026

The short version

Tidekeeper does not collect or transmit personal data. Your game progress and preferences stay on your device.

Data stored on your device

Tidekeeper stores game progress, settings, and preferences locally so your reef can continue between sessions. This information is not sent to the developer or to a developer-operated server.

Accounts, advertising, analytics, and tracking

Tidekeeper does not require an account and does not include advertising, behavioral tracking, third-party analytics, or third-party data-collection software development kits.

Device permissions and purchases

Tidekeeper does not request access to sensitive device data such as contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, or health information. The app does not offer in-app purchases.

Deletion

Deleting Tidekeeper removes its locally stored game progress and preferences from your device. Apple may separately retain App Store transaction and download records under Apple’s privacy policy.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here with a revised effective date. For privacy questions, use the Tidekeeper support page.
