Tidekeeper privacy policy.

Effective October 9, 2026

The short version

Tidekeeper does not collect or transmit personal or usage data. Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Data stored on your device

Tidekeeper stores game progress, settings, and preferences locally so your reef can continue between sessions. Tidekeeper does not send this information to the developer or to a developer-operated server. Deleting the app removes its locally stored game data from that device.

Accounts, advertising, analytics, and tracking

Tidekeeper does not require an account and does not use analytics or tracking technologies. The app binary includes the Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform software development kits, but advertising is disabled in this release: the app does not initialize these kits or request ads. Tidekeeper does not send personal or usage data to these kits in this release.

Device permissions and purchases

Tidekeeper does not request access to sensitive device data such as contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, or health information. The app does not offer in-app purchases.

Website cookies

This static support and privacy site does not use cookies or non-essential tracking.

Apple services

Apple may separately process information related to downloading or using the App Store under Apple’s privacy policy.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here with a revised effective date. For privacy questions, use the Tidekeeper support page or email tidekeeper@studiominden.org.
