Start in four steps

  1. Download the companion for your computer.
  2. Open it and follow the on-screen access steps.
  3. Keep your computer and iPhone on the same Wi-Fi.
  4. Open Wrapped on iPhone, choose Connect your computer, and enter the code shown.
