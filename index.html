Wrapped for Mac.

Bring selected Messages conversations to Wrapped on your iPhone over your local network. Your messages are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Download for Mac ↓

Apple silicon + Intel · Developer ID signed · Notarized by Apple · Free beta

Start in four steps

  1. Download and unzip the Mac companion.
  2. Move Wrapped Mac into Applications and open it.
  3. Use the button in the app to grant Full Disk Access.
  4. Open Wrapped on iPhone, choose Connect your Mac, and enter the code shown on the Mac.
