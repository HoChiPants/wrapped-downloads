Daily Dine support

Plan meals, organize recipes, and make grocery lists for the week.

Contact us

Email dailydine@studiominden.org. Include what you were trying to do and what happened. Avoid sending passwords or payment details.

Purchases and subscriptions

On iPhone, open Upgrade and choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Sign in to the same Daily Dine account used for that purchase.

Manage or cancel Apple subscriptions in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting your Daily Dine account does not cancel Apple billing. Web subscriptions are managed through Daily Dine.

Loading and offline recipes

Check your connection and use Try again if loading fails. Recipes you saved for offline use are available on the device where you saved them. Signing out or switching accounts clears those device copies.

Account and community

Delete your account and associated content through Profile → Account → Delete Account. To report a public note or block its author, use the menu beside that note.
