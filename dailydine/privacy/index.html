Daily Dine privacy policy

Updated September 21, 2026

Information we use

Daily Dine processes account details such as name, email, and user ID; recipes and images; meal plans; cookbooks; preferences; notes; community reports and blocks; and support messages. Subscription records include purchase status and transaction identifiers. Technical logs, device or browser information, and diagnostics may be processed to operate and improve the service.

Providers and sharing

Authentication, hosting, storage, analytics, error monitoring, email, and payment providers process information needed to provide their services. Apple handles iOS purchases, and Stripe handles eligible web purchases. We do not store full payment card numbers and do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when required by law or to protect the service.

Public content and device features

Published recipes, public notes, and shared meal-plan links may be visible to others. Camera and photo access is used when you choose recipe images. Saved offline recipes and dinner reminders are stored on your device and cleared on sign-out or account switching. Contact Support prepares a draft with app name, version, build, OS version, and device model; you choose whether to send it.

Retention and your choices

We retain data as needed to operate the service and meet legal obligations. Delete your account and associated Daily Dine content from Profile → Account → Delete Account, or email us with access, correction, or deletion requests. Apple subscriptions must be canceled separately through your Apple Account.

Children

Daily Dine is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Cookies

This static support site does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels. The Daily Dine application uses authentication and may use analytics and error-monitoring services. GitHub hosts this support site and may process technical request logs.
