Ward Cookbook support.

Updated October 14, 2026

Get help

Email cookbook@studiominden.org for sign-in, ward access, recipe contributions, or exports. Do not send passwords or private membership lists.

Join your cookbook

Sign in, then enter the code or follow an invitation provided by your cookbook leader. Signing in alone does not grant ward membership. Contact your leader about pending access.

Recipes, photos, and printing

Members can contribute recipes and optional photos within their authorized cookbook. Administrators moderate submissions and prepare cookbook exports. Check ingredients, allergies, and food-safety details before using a recipe, including imported recipes.

Account and content removal

Open Account and the profile controls to manage your sign-in account. Contact support for help removing account information or shared recipe contributions. Copies already downloaded or printed by others may remain.

Independent community service
