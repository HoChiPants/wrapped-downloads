Ward Cookbook privacy policy.

Updated October 14, 2026

Information in the service

Ward Cookbook stores account and membership information, including the verified email and profile details needed to identify contributors and manage ward access. When you contribute a recipe, we store the recipe text, contributor details, optional notes, and any photo you choose to save. Photos are optional; a saved photo is cropped, resized to at most 1200 × 900 pixels, re-encoded as JPEG, and stripped of EXIF metadata (including embedded location data) before storage.

Sensitive membership information

Ward or stake membership can reveal religious affiliation. This information is linked to your account and is used to control access to the cookbook you join. It is not sold or used for advertising.

How information is used

Information is used to provide sign-in, organize recipes within the appropriate ward or stake, support moderation, and prepare printable cookbook exports. The app does not sell personal information or use advertising or third-party analytics.

AI-assisted imports

If you choose the recipe import feature, the file or text you provide is sent to OpenAI to extract recipe details. Review imported information before saving it. Avoid uploading content you do not have permission to share. Information sent to OpenAI is subject to OpenAI’s terms and privacy practices.

Service providers and cookies

Clerk provides authentication, and hosting and database providers operate the service. The sign-in system may use essential cookies to maintain a secure session. The site does not use nonessential analytics or advertising cookies. Your theme preference may be saved in your browser’s local storage.

Visibility and retention

Recipes and membership details are available to people authorized for the corresponding cookbook scope. A recipe photo is shown with its recipe and may be included in an authorized PDF export. Authorized cookbook administrators can prepare exports and moderate submissions. Recipe information is retained while it is part of the cookbook service and may remain in exports already downloaded by others.

Requests and contact

For questions or requests about account or recipe information, contact cookbook@studiominden.org. We may need to verify your account and coordinate with the relevant cookbook administrator before changing shared cookbook records.

Support diagnostics

The native Contact Support action opens an email draft with app name, marketing version, integer build number, OS version, and device model. It does not silently attach logs, identifiers, account details, or recipe content.

This support site

This static GitHub Pages site does not use cookies or analytics. The separate cookbook service uses essential authentication cookies. GitHub Pages may process request information for hosting and security.
