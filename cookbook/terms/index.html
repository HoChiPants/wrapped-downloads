Ward Cookbook terms of use.

Updated October 14, 2026

Using Ward Cookbook

Use the service lawfully and follow the access rules for the ward or stake cookbook you join. Keep your sign-in details secure and do not try to access another cookbook without authorization. Ward Cookbook is an independent community service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Recipes and photos

You keep ownership of the material you contribute. You give Ward Cookbook and the cookbook administrators permission to store, display within the authorized cookbook scope, moderate, and include your contribution in cookbook exports. Only submit content and photos you own or have permission to share. If a photo includes another person, get their permission before uploading it. Contact support to request removal; copies already included in downloaded cookbooks may remain.

Review and imported content

Recipe imports and generated ingredient details can be incomplete or inaccurate. Check ingredients, quantities, instructions, allergies, and food-safety details before relying on them. Cookbook administrators may moderate, archive, or exclude submissions under their cookbook’s rules.

Service availability

The service and its features may change or become temporarily unavailable. Cookbook exports already downloaded by users are outside the service’s control.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to cookbook@studiominden.org.
